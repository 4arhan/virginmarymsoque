Jumu’ah & daily prayers

Jumu’ah is held in three congregations every Friday — 12:15 PM, 1:15 PM and 2:15 PM — with khutbahs in Arabic and English. A dedicated women’s prayer section is available.

Daily adhan and iqamah times follow the mosque’s official display at awqat.com.au/vmm and are shown on our daily prayers page.

Planning a visit

This masjid has served Melbourne’s west since 2006, and anyone who would like to see it or learn about Islam is warmly welcome. Please call or email ahead to arrange a visit outside prayer times, book a school or community group visit, or ask about access needs — see visitors & questions about Islam.

Curious about Islam and prefer to learn at your own pace? Our da’wah partner IntroToIslam offers free online courses.
