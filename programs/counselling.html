Pastoral care

When to reach out

People come to the Imam with all kinds of burdens, including:

  • Struggles with faith, prayer or doubt
  • Family or marital tension
  • Grief and bereavement
  • Anxiety, depression, or low mood
  • Substance use or gambling
  • Family violence (see below)
  • Identity, belonging, and life transitions

Conversations are confidential. Where specialist care is the right step, we will help you find a clinical psychologist or GP.

Family violence

If you are in danger

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, call 000. For confidential support:

Immediate danger 000 Safe Steps 1800 015 188 24/7, women 1800 RESPECT 1800 737 732 24/7, all genders Men’s Referral Service 1300 766 491

Our masjid takes family violence seriously. You can come to us and be believed, and we will help you find safety and support.
