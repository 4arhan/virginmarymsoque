Nikah (marriage) services

A sound Nikah, conducted with care, by an Imam who has known you before the day itself.

What we provide

  • Pre-marital consultation — with the Imam, covering the conditions of a sound Nikah, communication, and rights and responsibilities.
  • The Nikah ceremony — in the mosque, or at another venue by arrangement.
  • Civil registration — ask us about civil registration of your marriage when you enquire.
  • Nikah certificate — a certificate is provided for the couple.
