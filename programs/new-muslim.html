The declaration of faith

Taking your Shahada

When you are ready to declare your belief, the Imam or a trusted member of the congregation will walk you through it. Nothing is required of you but the words themselves — said in whatever language you are comfortable in — and we rejoice with you. If you would like a record of your Shahada, the office can advise.

أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ

“I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is the messenger of Allah.”
