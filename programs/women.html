Women’s programs

Led by and for the women of our congregation, in the tradition of a mosque named for Maryam (peace be upon her).

Women’s halaqa

Qur’an reflection, community tea and unhurried conversation.

Wellbeing circle

A supportive, confidential space for women to share and be heard.

Mothers’ group

Babies and toddlers welcome — playgroup, activities and peer support.

Book club

Reading and discussing books on faith, history and Muslim lives.

Marriage & family

Workshops and guidance for couples and families. See Nikah services.

Women’s prayer section

A dedicated women’s prayer section is available at the mosque.
