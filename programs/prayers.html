Prayer times & Jumu’ah

Five daily prayers, Friday Jumu’ah, and Eid and Ramadan prayers at 143 Hogans Rd, Hoppers Crossing.

Jumu’ah

  • Three sessions every Friday: 12:15 PM, 1:15 PM and 2:15 PM
  • Khutbahs are delivered in Arabic and English
  • A dedicated women’s prayer section is available

Special prayers

Ramadan & Eid

Taraweeh

Taraweeh prayers are held nightly after Isha throughout Ramadan — check announcements for details.

Eid prayers

Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha prayers are held at the mosque. Arrangements and times are announced on our Facebook and Instagram pages closer to the date.
