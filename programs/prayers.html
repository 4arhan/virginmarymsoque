Prayer times in your calendar

Subscribe once — every day’s times appear by themselves

Add the masjid’s calendar to your phone and each day’s adhan and iqamah times, and Friday’s Jumu’ah, show up alongside your own events — changing with the seasons without you doing a thing.

Google Calendar iPhone & Mac Outlook

Copied. In any calendar app, choose “Subscribe” or “Add from URL” and paste it.

How it works, and more options
  • Google Calendar: the button opens Google Calendar and asks to add the feed. If you are on a phone, do this once on a computer — the calendar then appears in the Google Calendar app too. (Manual route: Other calendars › + › From URL.)
  • iPhone, iPad & Mac: tap the button and choose Subscribe. Or: Settings › Calendar › Accounts › Add Subscribed Calendar, and paste the link.
  • Outlook: the button opens Outlook on the web; in Outlook desktop use Add calendar › Subscribe from web.
  • Only Jumu’ah? Use https://virgin-mary-mosque.vercel.app/prayer-times.ics?prayers=jumuah instead.
  • Reminders: add ?reminder=10 to the link for an alert 10 minutes before each prayer (Apple and Outlook; Google uses its own notification settings).

Feed address: https://virgin-mary-mosque.vercel.app/prayer-times.ics. Times update from the mosque display at awqat.com.au/vmm; calendar apps refresh subscribed feeds about once a day.
