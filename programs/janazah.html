Janazah (funeral) services

إِنَّا لِلَّٰهِ وَإِنَّا إِلَيْهِ رَاجِعُونَ — to Allah we belong, and to Him we return. In the hardest hours, we are here to help your family with care and dignity.

Need help now? Call the masjid on (03) 8087 0900 or email admin@virginmarymosque.org.au and we will walk you through the first steps.

Every step, handled with care

How we can help

  • Ghusl — we can help arrange the ritual washing of the deceased.
  • Kafan — guidance with shrouding arrangements.
  • Janazah prayer — held at the masjid; times are arranged with the family and announced to the community.
  • Burial — coordination with Muslim burial grounds across Melbourne.
  • Paperwork — guidance on documentation and liaison with funeral directors.

Practical matters

Costs

Ghusl and burial costs are set by the relevant providers — contact the office and we will explain what is involved. If cost is a barrier, please speak to us; no family should face this alone.

Grief & support

After the burial

Grief does not end at the graveside. Our community supports grieving families with condolence visits and ongoing pastoral care — please tell us what would help. You are also welcome to speak with the Imam at any time (see counselling & chaplaincy).
