Every step, handled with care

How we can help

  1. Ghusl

    We can help arrange the ritual washing of the deceased.

  2. Kafan

    Guidance with shrouding arrangements.

  3. Janazah prayer

    Held at the masjid; times are arranged with the family and announced to the community.

  4. Burial

    Coordination with Muslim burial grounds across Melbourne.

  • Paperwork Guidance on documentation and liaison with funeral directors.

Practical matters & support

Costs, and care after the burial

Practical matters

Costs

Ghusl and burial costs are set by the relevant providers — contact the office and we will explain what is involved. If cost is a barrier, please speak to us; no family should face this alone.

Grief & support

After the burial

Grief does not end at the graveside. Our community supports grieving families with condolence visits and ongoing pastoral care — please tell us what would help. You are also welcome to speak with the Imam at any time.
