Janazah (funeral) services

Inna lillahi wa inna ilayhi raji’un. We are here to help your family with care and dignity.

Need help now? Call the mosque on (03) 8087 0900 or email admin@virginmarymosque.org.au and we will walk you through the first steps.

How we can help

  • Ghusl — we can help arrange ghusl (ritual washing).
  • Kafan — guidance with kafan (shrouding) arrangements.
  • Janazah prayer — at the mosque; times are arranged with the family and announced to the community.
  • Burial — coordination with Muslim burial grounds across Melbourne.
  • Paperwork — guidance on paperwork and liaison with funeral directors.

Costs

Ghusl and burial costs are set by the relevant providers. If cost is a barrier, please speak to us.

After the burial

Grief does not end at the graveside. Our community can support grieving families with condolence visits and ongoing pastoral care — please tell us what would help. You are also welcome to speak with the Imam at any time (see counselling & chaplaincy).
