Why “Virgin Mary Mosque”?

Maryam (peace be upon her) is the only woman named in the Qur’an, and its nineteenth surah carries her name. Our masjid bears it in that light — as the Qur’an honours her.

Surah Maryam · Surah Al-Imran

Her name in the Qur’an

Maryam, the mother of the Prophet ‘Isa (peace be upon them both), is named dozens of times in the Qur’an — more than in any other scripture. Surah Maryam, the nineteenth chapter, narrates her devotion, her seclusion, the miraculous birth of her son, and the defence her infant offered of her honour. Surah Al-Imran tells of her own birth and upbringing in the family of ‘Imran, dedicated to the worship of Allah from before she was born.

يَـٰمَرْيَمُ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ ٱصْطَفَىٰكِ وَطَهَّرَكِ وَٱصْطَفَىٰكِ عَلَىٰ نِسَآءِ ٱلْعَـٰلَمِينَ

“O Maryam, indeed Allah has chosen you and purified you and chosen you above the women of the worlds.”

Qur’an 3:42

Her honour in Islam

Honoured in her own right

In Islam, Maryam (peace be upon her) is honoured in her own right: a woman of truthfulness, purity and complete devotion to Allah, chosen above the women of the worlds. Her story is not borrowed — it is Qur’anic revelation, and every Muslim who recites Surah Maryam keeps her memory alive in worship.

Our commitment

What the name asks of us

  • Worship: we are, first and always, a house of Allah — five daily prayers, Jumu’ah, Eid, and Qur’anic learning.
  • Hospitality: our doors are open. Anyone who wants to see the masjid, ask a question about Islam, or arrange a school or community group visit is warmly welcome.
  • Integrity: we teach Maryam’s story exactly as the Qur’an teaches it — nothing added, nothing softened.
  • Dignity: our masjid includes a dedicated women’s prayer section — a mosque named for a woman could settle for nothing less.
