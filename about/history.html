Our history

Serving Hoppers Crossing and Melbourne’s west since 2006.

Established 2006

The Virgin Mary Mosque (Masjid Maryam) was established in 2006 to serve the Muslim community of Melbourne’s west. Today the mosque stands at 143 Hogans Rd, Hoppers Crossing, in the City of Wyndham — a place of daily prayer, learning, and community life.

Each week the mosque hosts the five daily prayers and Jumu’ah — three sessions every Friday at 12:15 PM, 1:15 PM and 2:15 PM, with khutbahs in Arabic and English — alongside a dedicated women’s prayer section and ongoing community programs.

The name we carry

Our mosque is named for Maryam (peace be upon her) — honoured by Muslims and Christians alike. The story behind the name, and what it asks of us as a congregation, has a page of its own.
