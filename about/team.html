Our Imam

Our imam leads the congregation in the daily prayers and Jumu’ah, delivers khutbahs in Arabic and English, and is available for religious guidance and pastoral care.

To arrange a conversation with the imam, contact the office on (03) 8087 0900 or email admin@virginmarymosque.org.au.

How the masjid is run

The Virgin Mary Mosque (Masjid Maryam) has served the community since 2006, held in trust for the worshippers it serves. For governance enquiries, contact admin@virginmarymosque.org.au or call (03) 8087 0900.
