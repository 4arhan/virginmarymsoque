Why we give

Charity that outlives us

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ taught that when a person dies, their deeds come to an end except three: a continuing charity, beneficial knowledge, and a righteous child who prays for them. A masjid gathers all three — every prayer prayed, every ayah learned and every heart guided here is written, insha’Allah, for those who helped keep its doors open.

Not sure where you fit? Call the office on (03) 8087 0900 or email admin@virginmarymosque.org.au and we will help you find the right place.

“When the son of Adam dies, his deeds come to an end except three: a continuing charity, knowledge that benefits, or a righteous child who supplicates for him.”
