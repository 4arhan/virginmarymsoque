Volunteer with us

Our mosque runs on volunteers. Even a few hours a month can change what we are able to do.

Jumu’ah

Ushering & hospitality

Welcoming worshippers and visitors and helping Friday run smoothly across our three Jumu’ah sessions.

Education

Teaching & tutoring

Supporting our madrasah, adult classes and Arabic tutoring.

Community

Food & welfare support

Helping prepare and distribute food and practical support for local families in need.

Youth

Youth mentoring

Mentoring and youth activities. A Working with Children Check (WWCC) is required.

Interfaith

Interfaith team

Helping host visiting groups and interfaith events.

Skills-based

Skilled volunteering

Legal, accounting, design, IT, translation and more — lend your profession to the mosque.

Register your interest

Email or call the office and tell us a little about yourself and where you’d like to help — ushering, teaching, food and welfare, youth, interfaith, or professional skills. We’ll get back to you as soon as we can.
