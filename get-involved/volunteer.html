Good to know

Before you start

No experience is needed for most roles — we will show you the ropes. Tell us a little about yourself and where you’d like to help: ushering, teaching, food and welfare, youth, welcoming visitors, or professional skills.

A Working with Children Check (WWCC) is required for roles involving minors. The office can help you with the process.
