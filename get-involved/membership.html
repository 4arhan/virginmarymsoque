Belong to the masjid

Why become a member

Virgin Mary Mosque (Masjid Maryam) has served the Muslims of Melbourne’s west since 2006, and its members are the backbone of that work.

  • Support the masjid

    Membership supports the running of the masjid.

  • Belong to the community

    It connects you more closely to the community the masjid serves.

  • A stake in its future

    Take a formal stake in the future of this house of Allah.

The office will confirm the current membership types, fees, benefits and eligibility when you apply — and if cost is a concern, please speak to us.

Two minutes

How to apply

  1. Gather your details

    Your name, phone number, and whether you are applying as an individual, family or student.

  2. Send them to the office

    Email the office — or call and we’ll take your details over the phone.

  3. We’ll be in touch

    We’ll reply as soon as we can, and confirm current fees.
