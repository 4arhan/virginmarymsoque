Become a member

Membership is a practical way to stand behind this house of Allah and take a formal stake in its future.

Belong to the masjid

Why become a member

Virgin Mary Mosque (Masjid Maryam) has served the Muslims of Melbourne’s west since 2006, and its members are the backbone of that work. Membership supports the running of the masjid and connects you more closely to the community it serves.

The office will confirm the current membership types, fees, benefits and eligibility when you apply — and if cost is a concern, please speak to us.

You can also get involved by volunteering or donating.

Two minutes

How to apply

Email the office with your name, phone number and whether you are applying as an individual, family or student — or call and we’ll take your details over the phone.

We’ll reply as soon as we can.

For renewals, changes to your details, or any other membership questions, email admin@virginmarymosque.org.au.
