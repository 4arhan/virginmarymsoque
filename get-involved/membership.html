Become a member

Membership is a practical way to stand behind the mosque’s work and take a formal stake in its future.

Why become a member

Virgin Mary Mosque (Masjid Maryam) has served Melbourne’s west since 2006, and its members are the backbone of that work. Membership supports the running of the mosque and connects you more closely to the community.

Membership is open as Individual, Family or Student. The office will confirm current fees, benefits and eligibility when you apply — and if the fee is a concern, please speak to us.

You can also get involved by volunteering or donating.

Apply

Email the office with your name, phone number and whether you are applying as an individual, family or student — or call and we’ll take your details over the phone.

Email your application Call (03) 8087 0900

We’ll reply as soon as we can.
