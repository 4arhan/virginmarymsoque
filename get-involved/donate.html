Practicalities

How to give

  • In person: give at the mosque, or speak to the office for receipts.
  • Bank transfer: contact the office for current account details before transferring.
  • Bequests: considering a gift in your will? Contact the office to discuss.
  • In-kind: books, prayer rugs and other items — please contact us first.

Please contact the office about receipts and tax deductibility.

You can also support the masjid with your time — see volunteering and membership.
