Zakat calculator

Work out your zakat

Zakat is 2.5% of the wealth you have held for a full lunar year, once it is above the nisab.

  • The nisab

    The threshold is the value of 85 g of gold or 595 g of silver. Check today’s price, or ask the Imam which to use.

  • A lunar year

    Zakat is due on wealth held above the nisab for one Islamic (lunar) year.

  • Not sure?

    Jewellery, superannuation, business assets and debts can be complex — ask the Imam.

Good to know

Questions about giving

Is it safe to donate online?

Yes. Payments are processed by Stripe on its secure checkout page — your card details are entered there and never reach this website.

Will I get a receipt?

Stripe emails a receipt to the address you enter at checkout. For a receipt from the masjid, please contact the office.

Is my donation tax deductible?

Please contact the office about receipts and tax deductibility.

Can I give on behalf of someone?

Yes. At checkout, add their name in the “On behalf of” field — for example, a parent who has passed away.

How do I change or cancel a monthly donation?

Contact the office on (03) 8087 0900 or admin@virginmarymosque.org.au and we will update or cancel it for you.

How are the Gaza and emergency appeals distributed?

Donations to each appeal are recorded separately. For details of how an appeal is distributed, please contact the office.
