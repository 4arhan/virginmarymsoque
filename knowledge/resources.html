Privacy

Virgin Mary Mosque (Masjid Maryam) is committed to handling your personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles. We collect only the information we need to deliver our services, we do not sell your data, and you can ask for your information to be accessed, corrected or deleted at any time by emailing admin@virginmarymosque.org.au.

For any privacy questions, contact the mosque office on (03) 8087 0900.
