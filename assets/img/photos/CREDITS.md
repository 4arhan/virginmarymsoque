# Placeholder photography

Every photo in this folder is a **placeholder** of a mosque somewhere else in the world,
used until photos of Masjid Maryam itself are ready. All are from
[Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) and free to use under the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). Credit is shown on the site anyway.

## Replacing a placeholder with a real photo

Files are named by **where they appear** on the site, not by what they show. To replace one:

1. Export your photo as WebP at the width listed below (`-sm` files are the half-size
   version for phones). [Squoosh](https://squoosh.app) does this in the browser.
2. Save it over the old file, keeping the same name.
3. Update the caption/credit text next to the `<img>` in the HTML (search for the filename).
