Ask us about Islam

Every question is welcome — from the simplest to the hardest. Ask in person, by phone or email, or bring your class or community group to the masjid.

Photo: Spenser Sembrat / Unsplash

Every question welcome

There is no such thing as a bad question.

What do Muslims actually believe about God? Who was the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ? Why do Muslims pray the way they do? If you have wondered, ask — we would far rather you heard the answer from Muslims than from a rumour.

In person

Drop in and ask

Come by the masjid and a member of our community will happily sit with you. Friday afternoons around Jumu’ah are a lively time to visit — or arrange a quieter time with the office.
