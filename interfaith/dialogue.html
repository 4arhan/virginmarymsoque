Interfaith dialogue & events

Honest conversations and shared gatherings with our Christian, Jewish and multifaith neighbours in Melbourne’s west.

What we do

Gatherings across faith lines

A mosque named for Maryam (peace be upon her) has natural common ground with its neighbours of other faiths. Through the year, Virgin Mary Mosque (Masjid Maryam) hosts gatherings that build on it:

Joint lectures

Talks and panel conversations on shared themes — above all Maryam herself, honoured in both the Qur’an and the Gospel.

Shared iftars

In Ramadan we open our tables, and neighbours of all faiths are invited to break the fast alongside our congregation.

Scripture study

Small reading circles: a short passage from the Qur’an, a related passage from the Bible, and honest conversation about both.

For dates and details of upcoming gatherings, follow us on Facebook, call (03) 8087 0900, or email admin@virginmarymosque.org.au.

Partnerships

Work with us

We welcome partnerships with churches, synagogues, schools, universities, chaplaincies and community groups — whether that is a one-off lecture, a student visit with Q&A, or an ongoing dialogue between congregations.

If your parish, congregation, school or organisation would like to build something with us, we would love to hear from you. And if you are simply curious about stepping inside a mosque, start with our guide for visitors.
