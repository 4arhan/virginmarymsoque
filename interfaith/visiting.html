Visiting the mosque

Thinking of visiting for the first time? Here is what to expect, in plain language.

You are welcome

You do not need to be Muslim to step inside. School groups, parishioners, journalists, students and curious individuals are all welcome, and members of our community will happily answer practical questions. If you would like a guided first visit, contact us in advance on (03) 8087 0900 or admin@virginmarymosque.org.au and we will arrange a time.

Getting here

Virgin Mary Mosque (Masjid Maryam) is at 143 Hogans Rd, Hoppers Crossing VIC 3029, in Melbourne’s west. Get directions on Google Maps.

What to wear

Dress modestly — long trousers or a long skirt, and a shirt with sleeves that covers the shoulders. Women are asked to cover their hair with a scarf inside the prayer hall; a simple scarf is fine, and do ask if you need to borrow one.

Everyone removes their shoes before entering the carpeted prayer areas. Shoe racks are provided.

Where to enter

The main entrance welcomes everyone. A dedicated women’s prayer section is available — ask at the main entrance and someone will point the way. Men continue to the main prayer hall.

What happens in a prayer

Islamic prayer involves standing, bowing and prostrating. If you are visiting, you are welcome to sit quietly at the back of the hall and watch respectfully. There is no pressure to participate. The prayer is short — usually 5–10 minutes — and is led by the Imam.

On Fridays, Jumu’ah runs in three sessions — 12:15 PM, 1:15 PM and 2:15 PM — each beginning with a sermon (khutbah) before the congregational prayer. Khutbahs are delivered in Arabic and English.

Taking photographs

Photographs of the architecture are welcome. Please do not photograph people during prayer. If you are on assignment, please speak to the office first.

Food and drink

No food or drink inside the prayer hall (except water). Many attendees stay to chat after Friday prayer — you are welcome to join them.

Booking a group visit

For school classes, parishes, chaplaincies or other group visits, please book in advance so we can look after you properly. Arrange a visit
